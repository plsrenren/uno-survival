UNO SURVIVAL RULES

⚡ 1. 3-Card Starting Hand

Each player is dealt only 3 cards to keep hand cycles blistering fast. High tension right from turn one!

🎯 2. Standard Matching

Match cards by color or number/action on the center discard pile. Wild cards can be played on anything.

🏆 3. "Going Out" = Safety

As soon as you discard your final card, you win and exit the game. You are designated SAFE with your escape rank (1st Safe, 2nd Safe, etc.)!

🔄 4. Turn Continuation & Skipping

The game doesn't end when someone empties their hand! Play continues around the remaining players, seamlessly skipping over the seats of all players who are already safe.

📣 Calling UNO

When you're down to one card, tap CALL UNO! before anyone catches you. Until you do, any player can call you out and you draw 2. Calling UNO while holding more than one card costs you a penalty card.

💀 5. Elimination / Last Player Left

Play continues until only 1 player is left holding cards. That player becomes the designated loser (or receives a strike). In 3-Strikes mode, accumulate 3 strikes and you are permanently eliminated!

❌ 6. Knockout

Hold more than 5 cards at any moment and you are ELIMINATED from the round on the spot. Your seat is skipped like a safe player's. Turn it off in Table Settings.

👥 7. Up to 16 Players

Host tables up to 16 seats! Friends join with your room code, and bots fill any seats left open.
